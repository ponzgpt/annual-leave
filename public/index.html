Annual leave

Tap a weekday to mark it · tap again for a half day · a third time to clear. Weekends don't count.

Hours in lieu

Log extra hours or bank holidays worked. Every hours add one day to the balance; spend it by marking days as Time off in lieu.

    Print / save as PDF

    In the print dialog choose “Save as PDF”. Turn on “Background graphics” if the colours don't show.

    Settings

    Everything saves automatically and opens the same on any device with this link. Keep the link private.
